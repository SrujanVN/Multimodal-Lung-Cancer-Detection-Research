About the Project

The Early Lung Cancer Detection project leverages advanced medical imaging and data analysis to identify lung cancer at its earliest stages. By combining X-ray, CT scans, and clinical data, our tool assists healthcare professionals in making faster and more accurate diagnoses, ultimately improving patient outcomes through timely interventions.
