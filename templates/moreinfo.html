More About Lung Cancer

Lung cancer is a type of cancer that begins in the lungs. It's one of the leading causes of cancer deaths worldwide. There are two main types: Non-small cell lung cancer and Small cell lung cancer.

Symptoms
  • Persistent cough
  • Shortness of breath
  • Chest pain
  • Weight loss
Causes
  • Smoking (major risk factor)
  • Exposure to radon gas
  • Asbestos and other carcinogens
Prevention
  • Avoid smoking
  • Regular check-ups
  • Healthy lifestyle
